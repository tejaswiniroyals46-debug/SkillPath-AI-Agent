**SkillPath AI**

Your Career. Your Skills. Your Future.

**SkillPath AI** is an AI-powered career guidance and placement-readiness platform designed to help engineering students identify skill gaps, build practical skills, and prepare for career opportunities.

**Problem Statement :** 

Engineering students often struggle to identify the skills required for their desired careers, find suitable learning resources, gain practical experience, and prepare for placements.

**Our Solution :**

SkillPath AI brings career planning, skill assessment, personalized learning, practical challenges, and placement preparation together in one platform.

**Key Features :**

- **AI Career Mentor**: Career-related guidance and recommendations.
- **Skill Assessment**: Assess skills and identify areas for improvement.
- **Personalized Learning Hub**: Learning resources based on career goals.
- **Career Roadmap**: Plan learning activities toward a target career.
- **AI Resume Analyzer**: Get feedback to improve your resume.
- **AI Mock Interview**: Practise interview questions and receive answer feedback.
- **SkillForge Code Lab**: Practise coding.
- **Jobs & Internships**: Explore relevant opportunities.
- **XP & Achievements**: Track learning activities and progress.
- **AI Career Agent**: Connect career goals, assessment results, and learning progress to recommend next steps.

Feature availability and AI-generated results depend on the current implementation and service availability.

**How It Works:**

- Create an account and complete your profile.
- Select a career goal.
- Assess your current skills.
- Identify skill gaps and explore recommended learning activities.
- Practise through projects, coding challenges, and mock interviews.
- Track progress and work toward career readiness.

**Technology Stack :**

Python,
Streamlit,
SQLite,
Google Gemini API,
Jooble API for job opportunity discovery,
HTML/CSS for interface styling, where applicable.
